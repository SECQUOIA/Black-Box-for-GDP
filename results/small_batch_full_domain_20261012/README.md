# Small Batch full-domain DFO results — October 12, 2026

All 40 Dantzig runs completed on CPU 6 with numerical threads set to one.
The run took 56.817441 seconds including environment checks and worker startup.
Every run used the full original GDP domain `{1,2,3}^3`, three shared initial
points, and a total budget of 10 callbacks. Of the 27 configurations, 12 are
feasible and 15 are rejected during preprocessing. RBFOpt used MSRSM + sampling.

| Method | Target hits | Feasible calls | Infeasible calls | Median completion estimate (s) | TTS99 estimate (s) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| DSDA | 8/10 | 42 | 58 | 3.831 | 11.492 |
| RBFOpt | 5/10 | 63 | 37 | 5.082 | 35.574 |
| BAM 5.0.0 | 2/10 | 51 | 49 | 4.227 | 88.764 |
| FMQA-SA | 7/10 | 47 | 53 | 4.683 | 18.730 |

There are 400 ordered callbacks: 203 feasible, 197 infeasible. Seven DSDA
callbacks repeat a previously evaluated point; all seven are retained and charged.
All four methods have the optimum in their shared initial design for seeds 1
and 4. BAM's two target hits are those initial-design hits; it found no additional
target within the remaining seven-call budget. This is a target miss, not a
solver execution failure. The lookup target is 167427.65711470306 at `(2,2,1)`.

Completion estimates use `method time - lookup-call time + sum(point costs)`.
All 27 point costs come from the existing Dantzig GAMS/IPOPT-profile enumeration.
Infeasible points have positive preprocessing costs (0.044–0.048 s) and zero
solver time. This experiment measured DFO lookup searches and reused point
measurements; these are not measured online NLP runtimes or first-hit times.

TTS99 uses the median completion estimate across all ten runs, including misses,
multiplied by `ceil(log(0.01)/log(1-p))`. The observed success rates imply 3, 7,
21, and 4 independent restarts respectively. Ten seeds provide an empirical
estimate, not a statistical 99% confidence guarantee.
