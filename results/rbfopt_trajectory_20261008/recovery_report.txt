recovered_cases.csv: complete usable historical trajectories.
unresolved_cases.csv: cases whose entire callback path cannot be established from retained observations.
recovered_coordinates.csv: all individually proven coordinates, including partial cases; evaluation_index is 1-based, x/y are grid indices 0..30.
recovered_trajectories.jsonl: complete cases only, in original evaluation order.
unresolved_candidates.jsonl.gz: unresolved candidate coordinates or post-restart log rows.
ambiguity_witnesses.jsonl.gz: alternative sequences compatible with retained observations, NOT recovered historical coordinates.

Log elapsed times are rounded to 0.01 seconds and have a different origin from newly instrumented callback clocks. Do not treat them as precise callback timings.
Inference uses the accurate, serial RBFOpt 4.3 behavior: the first strict global minimum gives the returned best point, and pre-restart value rows correspond to objective calls. Final best-point indices are saved explicitly.
The saved unique-evaluation counts are equal to total evaluations in every original run; this justifies the injective matching constraint. Initial three points come from the frozen design audit.
Each claimed coordinate is forced under these retained constraints. A candidate set or ambiguity witness must not be used as an observed trajectory.
A partial case is not declared impossible to recover by any future method; additional evidence or replay might resolve it.
