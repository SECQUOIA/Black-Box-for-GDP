## Display formatting

Main 1: CSTR references 7 decimals, other references 4 decimals. Main 3: percentages 1 decimal. Main 4: time medians 2 decimals, small search components retain 3–4. Main 5: successful times 2 decimals; preserve M/U/T/* statuses. Main 6: h/10 with failure annotation. Main 7: 2 decimals and infinity for zero hits. Main 8: CSTR objective 7 decimals, other objectives 3; seconds 2; dagger/double dagger/TL retained. Supplement 6: 7 significant digits. S8–S13: exact binomial intervals printed to 2 decimals. S14/S15 retain objective digits and round residuals for display. All CSVs retain full precision.

Table 2: no algorithm is executed to reconstruct settings; all 560 original per-job dictionaries are exported with their source. Prose and mathematical typesetting remain manual. Supplement 1/5/6/7 are exports of frozen model/grid inventories, not new enumeration or model solves.

Historical raw jobs remain in their original ZIPs. `cited_files.json` maps displayed filenames and package paths with hashes; `provenance.json` identifies the supplied sources.
