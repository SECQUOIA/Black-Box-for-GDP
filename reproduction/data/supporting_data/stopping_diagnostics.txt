Stopping diagnostics — existing selected experiments

No optimizer or NLP was rerun. All diagnostics use the selected archived runs.
Budget is an operational classification: full callback budget reached; for Grid RBFOpt and FMQA no distinct native stop-reason field is stored. Their raw completed status and configured/actual counts are retained.
Explicit Grid DSDA target/local log reasons take precedence even if the final callback equals B; 1 target-stop and 10 local-stop runs end exactly at B.
Grid DSDA checks the target after a local-search run, not after every objective callback. Target-based stopping therefore does not imply zero post-target callbacks.
Native function-call-limit termination (BAM) is counted as budget, not double-counted as other native stopping. No other native early-stop or time-limit execution occurs in the selected records.
Process DSDA local classification is supported by completed status, stopped_early=False, final history stopped=True, and fewer than B callbacks; no benchmark target is passed to this local search.
Post-target callbacks = actual callbacks minus the first accepted target callback; misses and failures have null, not zero. Initialization and repeated calls are included.
These descriptive counts do not isolate a causal runtime effect. No runtime is prorated to infer first-target overhead; no alternative timing rankings are computed.
Results are pooled across distinct budgets/conditions. The condition-level groups preserve the original comparisons.
